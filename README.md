Bouquet Builder Version 1.0

Hello! I’m Lena, a florist and data analyst. I created the Bouquet Builder to bring together information that florists and flower enthusiasts in Jakarta might find useful, along with a simple bouquet builder for anyone who needs a little guidance when creating an arrangement.

Flowers can be expensive, but that doesn’t mean creating a beautiful bouquet has to be complicated or limited to just a few types of flowers. I wanted to explore how data could help people make smarter decisions when choosing flowers for their arrangements. I compiled pricing data from different flower wholesale suppliers and created a dashboard showing the prices and availability of different flowers throughout the year.

I also wanted floral arrangements to feel more accessible to beginners. The Bouquet Builder introduces the basic structure of a bouquet, common types of flowers and their colours, and some combinations that can work well together. The goal is to give beginners a starting point and help them understand how different flowers can come together in an arrangement.

P.S. The dataset is currently static and will be updated whenever I receive new data.

Version 1.5 is currently in progress, where I’m working on a feature that will allow the data to be updated automatically.
